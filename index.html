(02) — About

A little about me

I'm Zoha, a UI/UX designer who enjoys understanding how people interact with digital products. I focus on creating interfaces that are visually balanced, easy to navigate, and built around real user needs.

My approach combines user research, thoughtful wireframing, interactive prototyping, and usability-focused design to turn ideas into meaningful digital experiences.

(04) — Process

From idea to experience

A clear, repeatable process that keeps people at the centre of every decision.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand the users, their goals, the problem, and the project requirements.

  2. 02

    Define

    Organize insights, identify priorities, and establish a clear user journey.

  3. 03

    Design

    Develop wireframes, create visual interfaces, and build interactive prototypes.

  4. 04

    Refine

    Review usability, gather feedback, and improve the experience through iteration.
